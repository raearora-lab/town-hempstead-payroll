What the numbers mean

01

Source

SeeThroughNY’s municipal payroll database, derived from records supplied by the New York State and Local Retirement System.

02

Coverage

The reporting year ends March 31. Retirees and certain post-retirement workers may not appear under the source methodology.

03

Interpretation

“Total pay” is pensionable earnings, including overtime and other extras. It should not be described as an employee’s authorized base salary.
